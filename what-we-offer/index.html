For teens and adults ages 14+. We're an affirming, identity-informed practice for LGBTQ+ and neurodivergent people, people of color, and members of all historically marginalized communities.

Individual Therapy

Individual therapy offers a space to slow down and look closely at what's shaping how you feel, relate, and move through the world.

The work begins within an alliance we build together, one that rests on genuine acceptance, collaboration, and nonjudgment. Clay draws from psychodynamic, person-centered, existential, narrative, and dialectical behavior therapy (DBT) approaches.

What is psychodynamic therapy? Therapy support for OCD alongside ERP

Relationship Therapy

Relationship therapy offers a space for you and your partner to identify your strengths and challenges, and to understand the patterns that shape how you relate to each other.

It isn't only for relationships in crisis. It's open to LGBTQ+ couples and consensually non-monogamous relationships, and to anyone who wants to strengthen a relationship that's working.
